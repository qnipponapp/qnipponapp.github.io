Privacy Policy

Nihongo Onomatopoeia Quiz (iOS) — Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QNippon ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in the iOS app Nihongo Onomatopoeia Quiz (the "App").

1. Information Used by the App

The App provides Japanese onomatopoeia quizzes, a dictionary, learning progress, and optional PRO features. It uses information such as quiz progress, answer history, selected language, display preferences, and bookmarks to provide these features.

Some preferences and learning records may be stored on your device. The App also communicates with third-party services for advertising, consent management, and in-app purchases, as described below.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertising to eligible users. Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers (where permitted), IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes that may include ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization.

Advertising data processing depends on applicable law, your device settings, and the consent or privacy choices presented to you. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information for AdMob publishers.

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the App uses a consent-management process before requesting ads. Your available choices may depend on your region and applicable law. You may also manage advertising-related permissions through iOS Settings, including tracking permission where applicable.

Declining optional consent may affect the type of advertising shown, but does not prevent you from using the App's core learning features.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional PRO purchase that unlocks Bookmarks and Review Later. Purchases and restoration of purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment information according to its own privacy practices. The App checks purchase entitlements to determine whether PRO features are available.

For details, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by Google in connection with advertising and by Apple in connection with in-app purchases. We do not sell your quiz answers or bookmarks directly to advertisers. Third-party providers process information under their own policies and applicable agreements.

6. Data Retention and Your Choices

You can change supported learning preferences within the App. Removing the App may delete data stored only on your device; information held by third-party services is subject to those providers' retention and deletion policies. If you have a privacy question or deletion request, contact us using the support information below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general language learning. Advertising and any age-related safeguards must comply with applicable laws and platform requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the App, please contact us.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to support privacy and security. However, no electronic system or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its services change. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
