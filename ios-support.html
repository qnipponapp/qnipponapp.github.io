Nihongo Onomatopoeia Quiz – iOS Support

Welcome to the support page for Nihongo Onomatopoeia Quiz on iPhone and iPad.

Learn expressive Japanese words through four-choice quizzes, examples in context, a multilingual dictionary, and star-based progress tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dictionary free?

Yes. The onomatopoeia dictionary is available to all users, without a PRO upgrade.

What does PRO include?

PRO unlocks Bookmarks and Review Later, helping you save and revisit quiz questions.

How do I restore a previous PRO purchase?

Open the app's Settings screen and look for the Restore Purchases option. Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with your original purchase.

How can I change the app language or Japanese text display?

Open Settings in the app to explore available language and text-display options.

What if a quiz screen does not fit on my display?

The quiz layout adapts to different screen sizes and Display Zoom settings. If needed, scroll vertically to access the remaining content. If you encounter a layout problem, please report your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Troubleshooting

If you experience a problem, try closing and reopening the app and checking whether an update is available. If the issue persists, contact us with:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The app version and build number, if available
  • A short description of what happened
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue
