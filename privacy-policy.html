Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

QNippon App ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Nihongo Onomatopoeia Quiz mobile application for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use our application.

1. Information Collection and Use

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name or email address.

Quiz progress, scores, and application preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

While we do not directly collect personal information through user registration, the application uses third-party advertising services that may collect or process certain information as described below.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The application uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Depending on the device, applicable privacy laws, and the user's privacy choices, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted.
  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses and general location inferred from network information.
  • Device information, operating system version, and application information.
  • Advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and related technical data.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

On Android, advertising identifiers are subject to Android's privacy controls.

On iOS, access to Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency requirements where applicable.

For more information about how Google uses information from applications that use its services, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the application presents a consent message allowing users to accept, reject, or manage their advertising preferences.

Users can revisit their advertising privacy choices through the application's privacy settings where this functionality is available.

On Android, users can also manage advertising-related privacy controls through their device settings.

On iOS, users can manage application tracking permissions through the device's Settings application.

Declining personalized advertising consent does not necessarily prevent all advertisements from appearing. Non-personalized or limited advertisements may still be displayed where permitted.

4. Log Data and Technical Information

Third-party advertising services may collect technical information, including IP addresses, device characteristics, operating system versions, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and timestamps.

We do not independently operate a system for collecting personal usage logs through the application.

5. Data Retention and User Rights

Quiz progress, scores, and application preferences are stored locally on the user's device and can be reset using the application's available controls.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their respective data retention practices and applicable privacy requirements.

Depending on their location and applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of their personal information, or to object to certain processing activities.

Users may contact us with questions or requests concerning their privacy rights using the contact information below.

6. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other personal information directly from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about a child's personal information, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the application, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
